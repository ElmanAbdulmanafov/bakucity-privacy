# BaKuCity — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

This policy applies to the mobile game **BaKuCity** (Android package `com.elman.bakucity`).

## 1. Who we are

BaKuCity is developed and published by an individual developer ("we"). You can contact us by opening an issue at
https://github.com/ElmanAbdulmanafov/bakucity-privacy/issues

## 2. What data is processed

- **Online play.** When you use the online mode, the game uses Unity Gaming Services (Authentication and Relay). These services create an anonymous user identifier on your device and process your IP address in order to connect you to other players. No account, email address or phone number is required.
- **Player name.** The name you type in the online mode is shown to the other players in the same room during the session. It is not stored by us afterwards.
- **Game progress.** Your progress, settings and purchases of in-game items with in-game money are stored only on your device.

## 3. What we do not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, videos, files, payment information or advertising identifier. The game contains no advertising and no in-app purchases with real money.

## 4. Third parties

Online services are provided by Unity Technologies, which processes the data described above on our behalf. See the Unity privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

We do not sell your data and do not share it with anyone else.

## 5. Security

Data sent to Unity Gaming Services is encrypted in transit (TLS).

## 6. Children

BaKuCity is not directed at children. It contains violence and is intended for players aged 18 and over.

## 7. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes all data stored on your device. To request deletion of the anonymous online identifier, contact us through the link in section 1.

## 8. Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new effective date.
